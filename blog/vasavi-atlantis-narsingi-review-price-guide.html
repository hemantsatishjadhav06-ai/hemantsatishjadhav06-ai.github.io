Vasavi Atlantis Narsingi 2026: Ready-to-Move Price, Review & Landlord-Share Saving

By Neopolis Infra Developers · 2026-09-16

Vasavi Atlantis is a landmark, ready-to-move high-rise community by the Vasavi Group in Narsingi, West Hyderabad — on the Narsingi–Gachibowli belt — 2, 3 and 4 BHK apartments (flats) from about 1,250 to 3,330 sq.ft (with sky villas up to ~6,500 sq.ft), indicatively priced ₹0.94–3.16 crore, RERA-registered as P02400003398, with possession from December 2025. It is one of the corridor's most recognisable projects: eight towers of up to 45 floors and about 2,199 apartments across ~12 acres, ~70% open space and 50+ amenities — with Tower 7 rising to roughly 142 metres, among Hyderabad's tallest. This guide covers what you get, what it costs, and how to buy the same flat for 8–14% less.

The short version: Atlantis is the rare premium Narsingi address you can occupy or rent today rather than wait years for — a genuine differentiator against the corridor's under-construction towers. You see the exact flat, take possession now, and (for OC-received units) avoid GST. The smartest way in is still the landowner's share, which typically sells 8–14% below the builder rate for an identical flat.

Where is Vasavi Atlantis, and why Narsingi?

Vasavi Atlantis sits in Narsingi, on the western Outer Ring Road — minutes from the ORR and Gachibowli and a short run to the Financial District. Narsingi is one of West Hyderabad's most established residential corridors: new gated-community stock, quick office-core access, and prices that sit a notch below Kokapet for comparable space. For the sub-market, read our Narsingi buyer guide and the West Hyderabad 2026 price guide.

For a landmark, ready project like Atlantis, that location keeps the flats highly rentable to IT and finance professionals, supports resale demand, and — because it is a recognisable, tall-tower address — holds its identity in the market.

Is Vasavi Atlantis ready to move in?

Yes — possession is from December 2025, so Atlantis is a ready (or near-ready) community rather than a distant under-construction bet. That changes the buyer maths in three ways: you can move in or rent from day one, you see the exact flat before paying, and a completed unit that has received its occupancy certificate (OC) carries no GST — a real saving versus the 5% on under-construction homes. Always confirm the OC and the specific unit's status before you commit; our ready-to-move vs under-construction guide explains why this matters.

What does a flat in Vasavi Atlantis cost in 2026?

Indicatively, flats at Vasavi Atlantis run from about ₹0.94 crore to ₹3.16 crore, spanning 2 BHK through large 4 BHK, with sky villas above that. A 2 BHK sits near the entry of the range; large 3–4 BHK homes and sky villas near the top. These are indicative figures — with a ready project, the live price depends heavily on the exact floor, view and unit availability, so confirm the current number.

Because many units are completed, budget accordingly: the usual ~7.5% for stamp duty, registration and transfer, no GST on OC-received units (only under-construction stock attracts 5%), plus interiors. Our complete buying guide breaks down the full cost stack.

Configurations, sizes and who each suits

Atlantis spans 2, 3 and 4 BHK plus sky villas, so it fits the widest range of buyers of any project in this set. The 2 and smaller 3 BHK homes (from ~1,250 sq.ft) suit couples, small families and investors chasing immediate rental income near the office core. The larger 3–4 BHK homes and sky villas (up to ~6,500 sq.ft) target families upgrading into a long-term home and HNI buyers who want a landmark, high-floor address. Being ready, it is especially strong for anyone who needs to move in now or wants rent from day one.

Height, scale and amenities

At eight towers of up to 45 floors and ~2,199 apartments across ~12 acres, with ~70% open space and 50+ amenities, Atlantis is a full-scale landmark community — and Tower 7 at ~142 m gives it one of the tallest residential silhouettes in the city. Height means premium views and privacy on the upper floors; the large resident base and completed common areas mean you can actually inspect the amenities before buying rather than trust a brochure. Confirm per-sq.ft maintenance and the sinking-fund position of the association before you sign.

The smart-money move: buy the landowner's share of Atlantis

Here is the lever most buyers miss — and it applies even to a ready project. In almost every large Hyderabad development, a block of flats belongs to the landowner, not the builder, allocated under the joint-development agreement (JDA). Those units are physically identical to the builder's — same tower, same floor plans, same amenities — but typically priced 8–14% below the builder or resale rate. On a ready ₹1.8 crore flat, that is roughly ₹14–25 lakh saved on move-in day.

Is Vasavi Atlantis a good investment?

For a buyer who wants a Narsingi landmark without a wait, Atlantis has a strong case: it is ready or near-ready (so rental income can start now), it is beside the ORR and Gachibowli (deep rental demand), and it is a recognisable tall-tower address that holds identity in the market. The caveats: as a large, established community there is a sizeable resale pool within the same towers, so be selective on floor, view and facing for standout resale; and a ready flat enters at a completed-home price rather than an early-booking discount. Buying the landowner's share, 8–14% below rate, is what most improves the entry — and it stacks with the no-GST advantage of an OC-received unit.

How to buy safely at Vasavi Atlantis

Run the standard checks, plus the ready-project and landowner-share specifics. First, verify the project on the TS-RERA portal using P02400003398, and for a completed unit confirm the occupancy certificate (OC). Second, insist on a registered sale deed and verify the title chain; for a landowner-share unit, also confirm the registered JDA and the sharing/allocation agreement that assigns that specific flat to the landowner.
