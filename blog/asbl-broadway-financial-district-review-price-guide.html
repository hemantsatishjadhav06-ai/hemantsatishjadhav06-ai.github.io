ASBL Broadway Financial District 2026: Price, Review & Landlord-Share Saving

The smart-money move: buy the landowner's share of Broadway

Here is the lever most buyers miss. In almost every large Hyderabad project — Broadway included — a block of flats belongs to the landowner, not the builder, allocated under the joint-development agreement (JDA). Those units are physically identical to the builder's — same towers, same floor plans, same amenities — but usually priced 8–14% below the builder rate to sell. On a premium Financial District flat, that is a substantial saving on the same home, and at this early stage it stacks with the widest choice of unit.

How to buy safely at ASBL Broadway

Run the standard checks, plus two specific to this project. First, obtain the RERA registration number and verify it on the TS-RERA portal — the registration, approved plan and the registered completion date — since these are on request rather than published; never book a 2029-possession flat without confirming its RERA status. Second, insist on a registered sale deed and verify the title chain; for a landowner-share unit, also confirm the registered JDA and the sharing/allocation agreement that assigns that specific flat to the landowner.
