Rajapushpa Infina Phase 2 Manchirevula 2026: Price, Review & Landlord-Share Saving

By Neopolis Infra Developers · 2026-09-17

Rajapushpa Infina Phase 2 is the newer, ultra-luxury phase of Rajapushpa Properties' (Rajapushpa Group) landmark high-rise community in Manchirevula, West Hyderabad — also listed under Narsingi and sometimes spelled *Manchirevulu* — right beside the Financial District — large 3 and 4 BHK homes from about 3,080 to 5,725 sq.ft, indicatively priced ₹2.83–5.27 crore (roughly ₹9,200 per sq.ft), RERA-registered as P02400007500, with possession slated for November–December 2028. It rises as six towers of up to 55 floors — among the tallest in the corridor — across roughly 13–15 acres and about 1,522 homes. This guide covers Phase 2 specifically: what you get, what it costs, and how to buy the same flat for 8–14% less through the landowner's share.

The short version: Infina is a top-of-market address for buyers who want very large, high-floor luxury homes next to the Financial District, and can wait until late 2028. Phase 2 is where landowner-share inventory is currently available — the same towers and specifications, priced below the builder's list. It is RERA-registered by an established developer, which lowers the usual under-construction risk.

Where is Rajapushpa Infina, and why Manchirevula?

Rajapushpa Infina sits in Manchirevula, a fast-rising pocket on the western Outer Ring Road that is effectively next door to the Financial District while pricing below the priciest cores. It is a near-premium location — minutes from the office belt, the ORR and Gachibowli — that gives you the commute advantage without the very top corridor rate. For the sub-market, see our Manchirevula buyer guide and the Financial District flats price guide.

For a landmark project like Infina, that location supports resale demand, keeps the homes rentable to senior Financial District professionals, and — because these are large, high-floor luxury units — underpins the premium you pay.

What does a flat in Rajapushpa Infina Phase 2 cost in 2026?

Indicatively, Phase 2 homes run from about ₹2.83 crore to ₹5.27 crore, or roughly ₹9,200 per sq.ft on the quoted configurations — a reflection of the large floor plates (3,080–5,725 sq.ft) and premium high-floor positioning. These are indicative figures; the live price moves with tower, floor rise, view and offers, so confirm the current number before you commit.

On top of the flat price, budget the usual Telangana costs: about 7.5% for stamp duty, registration and transfer, 5% GST (under-construction), plus interiors and parking — a meaningful sum at this ticket size. Our complete guide to buying a flat in Hyderabad lays out the full stack.

Configurations, sizes and who Phase 2 suits

Phase 2 is an all-large 3 and 4 BHK product — 3,080 sq.ft at the entry, up to 5,725 sq.ft at the top — so it is aimed squarely at end-user families and senior professionals who want a spacious, high-floor forever home, and at HNI/NRI buyers who want a landmark asset next to the Financial District. These are not compact investor units; the value here is space, height, views and address. If you want a smaller ticket in the same corridor, a nearby project or a compact configuration elsewhere will fit better — but if you want top-of-market scale, Phase 2 is built for it.

Height, scale and amenities

At six towers of up to 55 floors across ~13–15 acres and ~1,522 homes, Infina is one of the taller, more amenity-heavy communities in the belt — pools, clubhouses, gyms, indoor games and yoga facilities are part of the offering. Great height and large floor plates mean premium views and privacy, but also make tower, floor and facing selection matter more than usual for both livability and resale. Confirm the exact amenity list, per-sq.ft maintenance and the phase-wise completion schedule in the agreement before you sign.

The smart-money move: buy the landowner's share in Phase 2

Here is the lever, and it is exactly where Phase 2 comes in. In almost every large Hyderabad project, a block of flats belongs to the landowner, not the builder — allocated under the joint-development agreement (JDA). At Infina, that landowner-share inventory is available in Phase II: the same towers, the same large floor plans, the same amenities — but typically priced 8–14% below the builder's rate. At this ticket size the saving is large: on a ₹3.5 crore flat, that is roughly ₹28–49 lakh off the same home.

Is Rajapushpa Infina Phase 2 a good investment?

For a buyer with a late-2028 horizon, Phase 2 has a strong case: a landmark, high-floor luxury product beside the Financial District, from a developer with a delivery record, and buying early gives an appreciation runway by hand-over. Possession is Nov–Dec 2028 — a genuine wait — so if you need a home or income sooner, compare with a ready option using our ready-to-move vs under-construction guide. Entering via the Phase 2 landowner share, 8–14% below list, is what most improves the maths at this price point.

How to buy safely at Rajapushpa Infina Phase 2

Run the standard checks, plus two specific to the landowner-share route. First, verify the project on the TS-RERA portal using P02400007500 — the registration, approved plan and the registered completion date for Phase 2. Second, insist on a registered sale deed and confirm the title chain; for a landowner-share unit, also verify the registered JDA and the sharing/allocation agreement that assigns that specific Phase 2 flat to the landowner.
